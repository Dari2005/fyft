Delete your Fyft account

  1. Open Fyft and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. The deletion happens immediately.

What is deleted

  • Your account, email address, username and profile picture
  • Events you published, with their photos and videos
  • Your "I'm going" plans (the going counts of those events drop)
  • Organizers and venues you follow, your notification settings and device push tokens
  • Reports you sent

Nothing is kept after deletion. People who were going to your events get a notification that the event was cancelled.

Can't sign in?

Use "Forgot password?" on the sign-in screen. If that does not work, write to contact email will be added before launch from the email address of your account and ask for deletion; it is done within 30 days.
