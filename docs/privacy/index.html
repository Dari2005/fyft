Fyft Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short: Fyft shows how many people are going to events in Skopje. Nobody can see who is going where: your "I'm going" is counted, never shown. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party tracking.

Who we are

Fyft is an independent app made in Skopje, North Macedonia. It is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: contact email will be added before launch.

What we collect and why

  • Account: your email address, username and password (stored hashed by our database provider, we never see it). If you use "Continue with Google", we receive your email address and name from Google. Used to sign you in.
  • Profile picture (optional): public, shown on your profile and events.
  • Events you publish: title, description, time, place, map location, capacity, photos and videos. These are public; that is their purpose.
  • "I'm going": stored so the app can count it, remind you and show your own plans. It is private: other users, organizers and venues only ever see the total number.
  • Who and which venues you follow: private. Only the number of followers is shown to others.
  • Reports you send about an event: seen only by Fyft, to review it.
  • Notifications: a push token for your device and your notification settings. If you turn on "New events near me", we save the location you set, only to match new events near it. It is never shown to anyone.
  • Your location on the phone: used on the device to show distances and centre the map. It is not stored unless you turn on "New events near me".

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Expo and Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging): delivering push notifications.
  • Google: "Continue with Google" sign-in and the map.
  • Komoot (Photon): when you search an address while creating an event, the text you type is sent to their address search.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this page and the pages that shared event links open.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Finished events stop being shown in the app but stay in the database as part of your account until you delete it.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, your events, your plans, who you follow, your notification settings and your photos. People going to your events are told they are cancelled. See also how to delete your account.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how it is used, by writing to the contact above. You can also complain to the Personal Data Protection Agency of North Macedonia (Агенција за заштита на личните податоци).

Age

Fyft is about nights out and is meant for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Important changes are announced in the app.
